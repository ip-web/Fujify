# Fujifilm Film Simulation Develop Presets for Adobe Lightroom & Camera Raw

These develop presets allow you to quickly apply all **20 authentic Fujifilm Film Simulation profiles** with a single click in Adobe Lightroom Classic, Adobe Lightroom (Cloud), and Adobe Camera Raw (ACR).

## ⚠️ Important Requirement
> **Photos must be converted to DNG using Fujifier beforehand.**
> 
> Fujifilm camera matching profiles are designed by Adobe specifically for Fujifilm cameras. Unconverted RAW files from Canon, Nikon, Sony, Panasonic, Leica, or Olympus will not display Fujifilm color science until Fujifier adjusts their camera calibration tags to Fujifilm X-T5. Once converted with Fujifier, all presets apply instantaneously.

---

## 🎨 Profile-Only Presets
Each preset modifies **only the camera profile**.
- **No changes to exposure, contrast, white balance, or tone curves**
- **Preserves all your existing adjustments, crops, and local masks**
- **Safe to click at any stage of your editing workflow**

## 🚀 How to Install

### Method 1: Automatic 1-Click Install in Fujifier (Pro)
1. Open **Fujifier**.
2. Go to **Settings** (Preferences).
3. Under **Lightroom Integration**, click **Install to Lightroom**.
4. The presets will be automatically copied into your Adobe CameraRaw settings directory.
5. Restart Adobe Lightroom Classic or Camera Raw. The presets appear under **Fujifilm Film Simulations (Fujifier)** in your Presets panel.

### Method 2: Manual Installation (Desktop)
Copy the `Fujifilm Film Simulations (Fujifier)` folder into:
- **Windows**: `%APPDATA%\Adobe\CameraRaw\Settings\`
- **macOS**: `~/Library/Application Support/Adobe/CameraRaw/Settings/`

### Method 3: Lightroom Mobile (iOS / Android / iPad)
1. In Fujifier, click **Export Presets (.zip)** (or zip the preset folder).
2. Transfer or AirDrop the `.zip` file to your mobile device.
3. Open Lightroom Mobile, select an image, navigate to **Presets**, tap the **`...`** (three dots) menu, and select **Import Presets**.
4. Select the `.zip` file.
